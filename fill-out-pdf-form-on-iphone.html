How to fill out a PDF form on iPhone

A school form, a job application or a visa form arrives as a PDF. You don't need to print it. Here is how to fill it in on your phone and send it back.

Updated November 2026

Free way: Markup in the Files app

  1. Save the PDF to Files (in Mail, touch and hold the attachment, then choose Save to Files).
  2. Open it in Files and tap the Markup (pen) button.
  3. Tap + and choose Add Text. Drag the text box onto the line and type. On newer iOS versions, some forms let you tap a field and type straight into it.
  4. Tap Done, then share the file.

This is fine for one or two fields. On long forms, placing and resizing each text box by hand gets slow, and the exact buttons differ between iOS versions.

Easier way: PDF Pencil

  1. Open the PDF in PDF Pencil. You can open files from Mail, iCloud Drive, Dropbox, OneDrive or Google Drive.
  2. Type your answers onto the form, tick the boxes, and add handwritten notes where you need them.
  3. Sign at the bottom with your finger or Apple Pencil (see how to sign a PDF).
  4. Save, then share it by email or message, or print it with AirPrint.

Tips for forms

  • Zoom in before you type, so your text sits neatly on the line.
  • Check the whole form once more before you sign. Undo fixes any mistake.
  • If the form is only on paper, scan it first, then fill it in.

Common questions

Can I fill a PDF form that has no fields to tap?

Yes. Many PDFs are just scanned pages with lines. You can still place text anywhere on the page.

Will the person I send it to see my answers?

Yes. Your text and signature are saved into the PDF, so they show in any PDF viewer, on any device.
