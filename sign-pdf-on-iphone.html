How to sign a PDF on iPhone

A contract, a rental agreement or a permission slip needs your signature. Here is how to sign it on your phone and send it back in a minute.

Updated November 2026

Free way: Markup signature

  1. Open the PDF in Files or from a Mail attachment, and tap the Markup (pen) button.
  2. Tap +, then Add Signature.
  3. Draw your signature, tap Done, and drag it onto the signature line.

Quick and free. Two things to know: iPhone keeps that signature for next time, and it is placed as a small drawing you resize by hand, so it can look thin or blurry when printed.

Sharper and private: PDF Pencil

  1. Open the PDF in PDF Pencil.
  2. Zoom in on the signature line and sign right on the page with your finger or Apple Pencil, like on paper.
  3. Pick your ink colour and thickness if you like. The app remembers them.
  4. Save and share.

Your signature is drawn as vector ink, so it stays sharp when zoomed or printed. It is never saved, not even inside the app, so nobody who picks up your phone can reuse it. The trade-off: you sign fresh each time, as you would on paper.

Common questions

Is a signature drawn on a phone legally valid?

In many countries a handwritten electronic signature is accepted for everyday agreements, but rules differ by country and by document. For legal, government or bank papers, check what the receiver accepts.

Can I sign with my finger?

Yes. Apple Pencil gives the most natural result on iPad, but a finger works well on iPhone, especially if you zoom in first.

Can I sign a paper document?

Yes. Scan it first, then sign the scan.
