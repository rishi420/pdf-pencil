How to write on a PDF with Apple Pencil or your finger

Correcting a draft, marking homework or adding notes to a contract: writing by hand is often faster than typing. Here is how to do it on iPad or iPhone.

Updated November 2026

Free way: Markup

  1. Open the PDF in Files and tap the Markup (pen) button.
  2. Choose a pen, pick a colour, and write on the page.
  3. Tap Done to save.

Good for quick notes. For longer writing, small handwriting can be hard to keep neat.

Like writing on paper: PDF Pencil

  1. Open the PDF in PDF Pencil.
  2. Zoom in where you want to write. Write small and neat with Apple Pencil or your finger.
  3. Choose ink colour and line thickness. Use undo and redo freely.
  4. Rearrange, copy or delete pages if you need to, then save and share.

PDF Pencil stores your writing as vector ink, not as a picture. That means it stays crisp at any zoom and when printed.

Get PDF Pencil on the App Store Free to download. The full version unlocks unlimited scanning and sharing.

Ideas for handwriting on PDFs

  • Teachers and parents: mark worksheets and homework sent as PDFs.
  • Students: annotate lecture slides and readings.
  • Work: add corrections to a draft contract before sending it back.

Common questions

Do I need an Apple Pencil?

No. A finger works on iPhone and iPad. Apple Pencil makes small, detailed writing easier on iPad.

Can I remove my notes later?

While you are editing, undo removes them step by step. Keep a copy of the original file if you want a clean version as well.
